About Me

I'm N'mah Seray Kamara, a multimedia and broadcasting student from Freetown, Sierra Leone.

I'm passionate about using media to tell meaningful stories, communicate ideas, and connect people. I'm currently developing my creative abilities through photography, videography, writing, broadcasting, and digital content creation.
